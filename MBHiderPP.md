# Privacy Policy for ManageBac Grade Hider Chrome Extension

Last Updated: April 2, 2026

## Introduction

Welcome to ManageBac Grade Hider​ ("the Extension"), a Chrome browser extension designed exclusively to enhance user experience by hiding grade information on the ManageBac platform. This Privacy Policy outlines our commitment to transparency and user privacy, with a core guarantee: the Extension does not collect, store, or transmit any user data—ever.

Our mission is to provide a simple, local tool for managing your ManageBac view, and we achieve this by operating entirely within your browser’s sandbox. Below, we detail how the Extension works and why your privacy is protected by design.

## Core Data Processing Principles

The Extension’s functionality is built on three non-negotiable principles:

### 1. Local HTML Modification Only​

The Extension interacts exclusivelywith the local HTML content​ of ManageBac webpages loaded in your browser. Its sole action is to apply CSS/JavaScript rules that hide grade elements (e.g., score tables, feedback fields) from your view.
No server-side processing: All modifications happen in real time on your device—no data is sent to our servers or any third party.
No persistent changes: Once you close the ManageBac tab or disable the Extension, the original HTML is restored.
### 2. No External Connections​

The Extension does not initiate any external network requests, including:
API calls to ManageBac or other services;
Analytics or tracking scripts;
Updates (handled via Chrome’s built-in Web Store mechanism, which does not share user data);
Advertisements or affiliate links.
This means the Extension cannot "phone home" or communicate with external systems—your activity remains entirely private.

### 3. No Data Collection (and Cannot Collect)​

We have deliberately designed the Extension to lack any data collection capabilities. Specifically:
We do not​ access, store, or process:
Personal information (name, email, account details);
Browsing history (beyond the active ManageBac tab);
Device identifiers (IP address, browser type, OS);
Grade data (even hidden grades are never saved).
The Extension’s permissions (e.g., "access to webpage content") are strictly limited to modifying the ManageBac UI—they cannot be used to extract or transmit data.
## Data Security

Since the Extension does not collect or transmit data, there is no risk of data breaches or unauthorized access. For added clarity:
All operations occur within Chrome’s secure sandbox environment.
No cookies, caches, or local storage are used to retain information.
## User Rights

Because we do not collect data, you have no data to request access to, modify, or delete. However, you retain full control over the Extension:
Enable/disable it at any time via Chrome’s Extensions menu.
Uninstall it to remove all traces (no residual files remain).
